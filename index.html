Neverland Ventures ("Neverland") is a global development and investment platform focused on experiential real estate and operating businesses.

Our development and investment approach is rooted in creativity and unconventional thinking, executed with institutional rigor. We partner with inspired founders, creators, operators, and brands, deploying a flexible combination of in-house and trusted institutional capital to bring compelling concepts to life—and scale them responsibly over time. Our investments span the capital stack and business lifecycle and are structured to support a wide range of ownership and organizational configurations.

The platform is being seeded with several flagship entertainment and hospitality projects currently underway in the southeastern U.S. and the U.K., alongside an investment in a global expeditions company. Neverland is actively pursuing additional investment opportunities.
