Neverland Ventures

Neverland Ventures ("Neverland") is a global investment platform focused on developing, managing, and investing in real estate and operating businesses engineered around experience and community.

We partner with inspired founders, creators, operators, and brands, deploying a flexible combination of in-house and trusted institutional capital to bring compelling concepts to life—and scale them responsibly over time. We support ideas that extend beyond conventional models while maintaining institutional rigor. Our investments span the capital stack and business lifecycle and are structured to support a wide range of ownership and organizational configurations.

Neverland is built on a trend many of us are witnessing firsthand: despite the surge in digital prominence over the past few decades, consumers are increasingly drawn toward meaningful, real-world experiences and community—particularly in outdoor settings. As a result, attractive investment opportunities are emerging where offerings remain fragmented, capital-constrained, or lack the infrastructure required to scale.

The platform is being seeded with several flagship entertainment and hospitality projects currently underway in the southeastern U.S. and the U.K., alongside an investment in a global expeditions company. Neverland is actively pursuing additional investment opportunities.
