Track Record

Since 2019, the principals of Neverland Ventures have, through legacy investment platforms, led the sourcing, underwriting, acquisition, development, repositioning, and disposition of more than $1 billion in hospitality and entertainment real estate throughout the United States.

Representative projects include 1 Hotel Nashville, The Ryder Hotel Charleston, Hotel Bella Grace Charleston, Viewline Resort Snowmass, Sheraton Denver Downtown, Embassy Suites Nashville, and Mark Lane Apartments Naples, among others.
